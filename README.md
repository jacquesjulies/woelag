# Woelag.fun — Natural Health & Wellness

**Discover natural wellness. Explore products. Make informed choices.**

Woelag.fun is a South African health and wellness affiliate website dedicated to helping visitors discover natural health products, explore wellness topics, and read informative product reviews and guides.

Our goal is to make exploring natural health, personal care, supplements, herbal products, and everyday wellness simpler and more accessible.

## 🌿 About the Project

Woelag.fun brings together product discovery, educational blog articles, and affiliate product recommendations in one easy-to-navigate website.

The website is designed with a clean, modern appearance, natural-inspired colours, responsive layouts, and clear calls to action.

## ✨ Features

- **Product catalogue:** Browse health, wellness, personal care, and natural products.
- **Product categories:** Explore products by category.
- **Product reviews:** Read about product ingredients, potential benefits, and available evidence.
- **Wellness blog:** Discover informative articles and practical guides.
- **Product images:** Explore products through visual previews and clickable product cards.
- **Search and filtering:** Find relevant products and articles more easily.
- **Affiliate links:** Visit participating retailers through affiliate links.
- **Responsive design:** Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **SEO-friendly structure:** Built with discoverability and long-term organic traffic in mind.

The repository contains the website pages, styling, scripts, product information, blog content, and image assets.

Typical files and folders include:

- `blog.html` — Blog articles and featured content
