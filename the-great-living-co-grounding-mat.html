The Great Living Co. Grounding Mat: What Does the Evidence Say?

Grounding mats are designed to create an electrical connection between the body and earth while you rest indoors. The idea is intriguing, but claims about sleep, pain, inflammation and circulation deserve a closer look before you decide whether this product is right for you.

Independent product research · Updated October 2026

The short answer: The Great Living Co. mat is a conductive-surface product, not a proven medical treatment. A small body of preliminary research has reported possible benefits from grounding, including a 2025 pilot trial on sleep, but the evidence is not strong or extensive enough to conclude that this specific mat treats inflammation, joint pain, anxiety, circulation problems or immune conditions.

What does “grounding” mean?

Grounding, also called earthing, generally means making conductive contact with the Earth's electrical ground. Outdoors, this can happen through direct contact with the ground. Indoor grounding products use a conductive surface and a cord intended to connect to a verified earth connection. The proposed health explanation is that this contact changes the body's electrical state in ways that might influence biological processes. The mechanism and clinical importance of these proposed effects remain uncertain.

What does the research say?

Sleep and stress

A small randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot study published in 2025 assigned 60 participants to use either a grounding mat or a non-grounding control mat for six hours daily over 31 days. The authors reported improvements in several self-reported sleep and stress measures in the grounding group, along with a change in measured total sleep time. This is a useful early signal, but a single small pilot study cannot establish that grounding mats reliably improve sleep for everyone. It also does not prove that this specific brand produces the same outcomes.

Pain, inflammation and circulation

Some small studies and a 2020 review have suggested possible changes in pain, inflammation-related measurements, blood flow or other outcomes. However, the research base is limited, and some publications are strongly supportive of grounding despite important questions about study size, design, independence and replication. Larger, well-designed studies by independent research teams are needed before these claims can be treated as established clinical benefits.

Immune function and healing

The retailer's listing makes broader claims about immune function and healing. The evidence reviewed here does not establish that this mat improves immune function, speeds recovery from illness or injury, or treats an underlying medical condition. It should not be marketed or used as a substitute for medical care.

How to interpret the claims: Early research can justify further study; it does not make a benefit certain. Research on grounding as a general practice also cannot automatically validate every grounding product, its electrical setup or its advertised health claims.

Electrical safety matters

Because this product comes with a cord and plug adapter, setup deserves careful attention. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and use only a verified, properly earthed connection as specified for the product. Do not modify plugs, improvise a connection to household wiring, or assume that an outlet is correctly earthed because it looks normal. If you are unsure about the outlet or the supplied components, do not connect the mat; ask a qualified electrician to check the installation. Keep the cord and adapter in good condition and stop using the product if any component is damaged.

If you use medical equipment or have a condition that makes electrical safety a particular concern, ask an appropriate healthcare professional before use. Do not disconnect or alter any safety feature of other electrical devices to accommodate a grounding mat.

Who should consider it?

An adult who is curious about grounding may view the mat as an optional wellness accessory, provided it is set up safely and expectations remain realistic. It is not necessary for health, and there is no reliable basis to promise that it will improve a person's sleep, pain, stress or circulation. If you are experiencing persistent pain, poor sleep, unusual swelling, circulation symptoms or other ongoing health concerns, seek medical advice rather than relying on the mat.

Care and practical considerations

  • Confirm the dimensions and whether it suits your bed or intended surface.
  • Read all instructions and check the cord, adapter and test pen before use.
  • Spot-clean gently with mild soap and a damp cloth, then let it dry fully.
  • Do not machine-wash, iron or use harsh cleaning products.
  • Check current price, stock and returns terms before purchasing.

Is it worth the price?

At the listed price of R1,499, the main question is whether you value trying a grounding accessory despite uncertain health benefits. Consider it a comfort or wellness experiment rather than a treatment. If your main goal is better sleep, established habits such as a consistent sleep schedule, a comfortable sleep environment and professional help for persistent insomnia have a stronger practical foundation.
