Metagenics Concentrated Ultra Prostagen: Ingredients, Research and Safety

Prostate supplements are often marketed for urinary symptoms associated with benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), a non-cancerous enlargement of the prostate. This guide looks at the formula's ingredients and what independent research can—and cannot—tell us about their benefits.

Product format: 60 capsules · Label serving: one capsule · Intended for adults

The short version: Prostagen combines saw palmetto and stinging nettle with vitamin D, vitamin B6, zinc, amino acids and lycopene. Some studies of certain herbal preparations have reported possible changes in urinary symptoms, but the overall evidence is mixed and product-specific proof is lacking. This supplement should not replace medical assessment or prescribed treatment for prostate or urinary symptoms.

What does the science say?

Saw palmetto

Saw palmetto is widely studied and promoted for lower urinary tract symptoms associated with BPH. However, evidence is inconsistent. The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) summarises a 2023 review of 27 studies and concludes that saw palmetto used alone provides little or no benefit for BPH symptoms. Results from one extract or formula should not automatically be applied to another.

Stinging nettle root

Some studies suggest stinging nettle, alone or combined with saw palmetto, might improve certain urinary symptoms. But the evidence is limited, preparations differ, and it is not enough to establish that this specific product works. Evidence about one herbal preparation cannot reliably predict the effects of every supplement containing the same plant.

Lycopene, zinc and vitamins

Lycopene has been investigated in prostate-health research, but NCCIH says evidence is insufficient to support it for preventing or treating BPH. Zinc and vitamins have recognised nutritional roles, but that does not mean taking extra amounts will reduce prostate enlargement or improve urine flow in someone who is not deficient. The small quantities of amino acids in this formula should not be assumed to produce a clinical prostate benefit.

What symptoms should not be ignored?

Frequent urination, urgency, a weak stream, difficulty starting, dribbling or waking repeatedly at night can have several causes. BPH is one possibility, but urinary symptoms can also be related to infection, medication effects, bladder problems or other conditions. A healthcare professional can assess symptoms and discuss appropriate monitoring or treatment options.

Seek urgent medical care if you cannot pass urine at all. Also seek prompt medical advice for blood in the urine, fever or chills with urinary symptoms, or significant pain. Do not delay care while trying a supplement.

Safety considerations

  • The manufacturer's professional information lists possible side effects including gastrointestinal discomfort, headache and a metallic taste. Stop taking the product and seek advice if you experience an adverse reaction.
  • The label advises people taking medication to consult a healthcare practitioner before use. This is particularly important when you take prescription medicines or have an ongoing medical condition.
  • Do not use a supplement as a replacement for prescribed BPH treatment or follow-up appointments.
  • Tell your clinician about all supplements you use, including before prostate testing, procedures or surgery.
  • This product is intended for adults; it is not a general wellness supplement for children or teenagers.

Our take

Concentrated Ultra Prostagen contains ingredients commonly found in prostate-support supplements, but the evidence does not establish that this specific combination reliably treats BPH or urinary symptoms. If symptoms are affecting sleep or daily life, a proper assessment is a more dependable first step than choosing a supplement based on broad marketing claims.
