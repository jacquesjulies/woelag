Men's wellness: make informed choices

Supplements and wellness products are often marketed with bold promises. Before choosing one, look carefully at the ingredient list, serving size, warnings, evidence for advertised benefits and whether the product could interact with medication or an existing condition.

Questions to ask before buying

  • Are the ingredients and amounts clearly listed?
  • Are the claims supported by reliable evidence, or are they only marketing language?
  • Are there warnings, side effects or medication interactions to consider?
  • Is the seller clear about price, delivery, returns and subscriptions?

Persistent concerns about sexual function, fertility, energy or urinary symptoms deserve advice from a qualified healthcare professional. A supplement is not a substitute for assessment or prescribed treatment.
