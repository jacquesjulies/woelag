Women's wellness: information that puts you first

Women's wellness needs can vary across life stages. When considering a supplement or personal-care product, read the complete ingredient list, check warnings and evidence, and consider whether it is appropriate for your individual circumstances.

Questions to ask before buying

  • Are the ingredients, amounts and directions clearly listed?
  • Are claims about hormones, fertility or menstrual health supported by reliable evidence?
  • Could the product interact with medication, pregnancy, breastfeeding or a health condition?
  • Are price, delivery, returns and any recurring subscription clearly explained?

Changes in menstrual cycles, fertility concerns, persistent symptoms or questions about reproductive health are best discussed with a qualified healthcare professional. Wellness products and tracking apps do not replace medical care.
