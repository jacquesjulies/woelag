Viridian Fertility for Men: Ingredients, Evidence and Safety

Male fertility depends on many factors, and nutrients are only one part of the picture. Viridian High Potency Fertility for Men combines minerals, vitamins and other ingredients marketed to support reproductive health. Here's what the listed formula tells us—and what it cannot promise.

The short answer: Some nutrients in this formula have established roles in normal body function. Selenium contributes to normal sperm formation, while vitamin C contributes to normal immune function and helps with iron absorption. These approved nutrient functions do not mean this specific supplement has been shown to improve fertility outcomes or treat infertility.

What's in the formula?

Based on the product information supplied for this guide, each listed formula includes the following ingredients and amounts:

  • L-carnitine — 50 mg: involved in energy metabolism; it has been studied in male fertility, but results from research on supplements vary and cannot guarantee an individual benefit.
  • Zinc citrate — 13 mg: zinc supports normal reproductive function and many enzymes. More is not always better, particularly when combined with other supplements.
  • Selenium (methionine) — 50 µg: selenium contributes to normal sperm formation and thyroid function. Excessive total selenium intake can be harmful.
  • Copper citrate — 500 µg: copper contributes to normal energy metabolism and other physiological functions.
  • Vitamin C — 25 mg and vitamin E — 34 mg: antioxidant vitamins involved in normal physiological functions. Antioxidant supplementation has been studied in fertility, but the evidence does not establish that every formula improves pregnancy or live-birth rates.
  • Folic acid — 100 µg and vitamin B12 — 26 µg total: contribute to normal blood formation and other functions; B12 is also involved in nervous-system function.
  • Co-enzyme Q10 — 15 mg and L-arginine — 50 mg: ingredients involved in cellular energy processes and nitric-oxide pathways, respectively. Evidence for fertility benefits depends on the ingredient, dose and study and should not be assumed for this product.
  • Other listed ingredients: natural beta-carotene, citrus bioflavonoids, pine bark extract (95% OPC), and a base of bilberry, spirulina and alfalfa.

The ingredient amounts above reflect the product information provided and should be checked against the current package. The product is described as vegan, free from artificial colours and preservatives, kosher, palm-oil-free and packaged in a recyclable glass bottle. It is made in the United Kingdom.

What does the evidence say about male fertility?

Male fertility can be affected by sperm count, movement and shape, hormones, genetics, infections, lifestyle factors and underlying medical conditions. Some studies have explored antioxidants and nutrients in men with fertility concerns, but research findings are mixed and the certainty of evidence varies. A supplement label alone cannot establish that a product improves conception rates or prevents infertility.

Selenium has a recognised role in normal sperm formation, and correcting a nutrient deficiency can be important for health. That is different from showing that taking extra selenium or a multi-ingredient supplement improves fertility in someone who already gets enough. Avoid combining several fertility or antioxidant supplements without reviewing their total doses.

Who should take extra care?

  • If you take prescription medication, have an underlying condition or are under medical supervision, ask your GP or pharmacist before using the product.
  • Check all other supplements you take to avoid unnecessarily high combined doses of selenium, zinc, vitamin E or other nutrients.
  • If you and your partner have been trying to conceive without success, a healthcare professional can advise on appropriate fertility assessment rather than relying on supplements alone.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and keep out of reach of children.

Our takeaway

Viridian Fertility for Men contains several nutrients involved in normal reproductive and general health, including selenium, zinc, folate and vitamins C and E. However, nutrient functions are not proof that this particular combination improves fertility. Use the current label to check amounts, avoid doubling up on supplements, and seek a proper assessment if fertility is a concern.
