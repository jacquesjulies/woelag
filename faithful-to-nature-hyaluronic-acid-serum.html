Product overview

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant used in skincare because it helps attract and hold water at the skin's surface. It is commonly included in moisturising routines to help skin feel hydrated and supple.

What to consider

  • Review the full ingredient list and directions for use.
  • Consider how the serum fits with your existing cleanser and moisturiser.
  • Introduce new skincare products gradually, especially if your skin is sensitive.

Results vary by formulation and individual skin. A serum is not a treatment for every skin concern, and product claims should be assessed in context.

Practical tip: Follow the retailer's directions and stop using a product if it causes persistent irritation. For ongoing skin concerns, ask a qualified healthcare professional.
