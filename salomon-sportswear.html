What to consider before choosing

  • Activity: Choose clothing suited to how you plan to use it—running, trail running, hiking or everyday wear.
  • Weather: Consider ventilation, rain protection and layering for the conditions you expect.
  • Fit and comfort: Check the retailer's size guide and product-specific fit notes.
  • Care: Follow the garment's washing and care label to help maintain its materials.
  • Value: Compare fabric, features, durability, price and return terms rather than relying on a product name alone.
