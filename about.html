About us

A more considered way to explore wellbeing.

Woelag is an independent information and product-discovery website focused on natural living, everyday wellness and selected wellness technology.

We aim to help readers ask better questions about products: what the label says, what evidence supports a claim, what a device actually measures, and what costs or limitations may apply.

How we work

  • We do not manufacture, sell, ship or fulfil products featured through third-party links.
  • Product specifications and availability can change, so readers should confirm details with the seller before purchasing.
  • We aim to avoid unsupported medical claims and do not promise health outcomes.
  • Some links are affiliate links and may earn us a commission if a qualifying purchase is made.
Health information: Website content is for general informational purposes only and is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Contact

For questions about this website or its content, use the contact details provided by the website owner where available. We do not publish an unverified email address.
