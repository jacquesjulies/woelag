Editorial policy

Woelag aims to provide clear, useful and responsible information about natural-living products, personal wellness and selected wearable technology.

Product information

Retailers and manufacturers may change ingredients, features, prices, subscriptions, availability and terms. Readers should verify these details with the seller before purchasing. Prices or ratings should not be published unless they have been checked and dated.

Claims and evidence

We aim to distinguish a manufacturer's description from independently established evidence. We do not promise that a supplement will treat a condition, alter hormones, improve fertility or deliver a particular outcome. Wearable devices are not presented as diagnostic tools unless their approved intended use clearly supports that description.

Affiliate relationships

Some links may earn Woelag a commission. Commercial relationships should be disclosed near relevant links and do not justify misleading claims.

Corrections

If you notice inaccurate or outdated information, contact the website owner through the contact details provided by the site where available so it can be reviewed.
